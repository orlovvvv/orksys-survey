### Analytics Features and Widgets in Survey and Feedback SaaS

* **Net Promoter Score (NPS) Widget**
* **What it shows:** Overall NPS score (-100 to +100), percentage distribution of Promoters, Passives, and Detractors, and historical trend lines.
* **Display controls:** Date range selectors, rolling average toggles, and user segment filters (e.g., by plan tier or role).


* **CSAT (Customer Satisfaction) and CES (Customer Effort Score) Trackers**
* **What it shows:** Average ratings (e.g., 1-5 or 1-7 scales), score distributions, and performance changes across specific product touchpoints.
* **Display controls:** Trigger-event filters (e.g., post-onboarding, post-support ticket), date parameters, and demographic breakdowns.


* **Sentiment Analysis and Natural Language Processing (NLP) Module**
* **What it shows:** Emotional categorization (positive, neutral, negative, mixed) of unstructured, open-ended feedback. Includes word clouds and thematic clustering (e.g., "pricing," "bugs").
* **Display controls:** Keyword search bars, sentiment-specific toggles to isolate data pools, and drill-down links to view raw verbatim responses.


* **Cohort Analysis Table**
* **What it shows:** Retention, engagement, and satisfaction rates of specific user groups over time based on shared characteristics or actions.
* **Display controls:** Cohort definition parameters (e.g., sign-up date, location), time intervals (daily, weekly, monthly), and metric selectors.


* **Heatmaps**
* **What it shows:** Aggregated visual overlays indicating where users click, scroll, hover, or experience friction on specific pages or survey interfaces.
* **Display controls:** Device type toggles (desktop vs. mobile), URL path parameters, and interaction-type selectors (click vs. scroll).


* **Session Replay Viewer**
* **What it shows:** Video-like playbacks of individual user interactions and navigations during the feedback or product usage process.
* **Display controls:** Playback speed adjustments, timeline scrubbers, and filters to skip inactivity or highlight rage-clicks.


* **Funnel and Journey Analytics**
* **What it shows:** Drop-off rates, conversion rates, and completion times across a series of predefined steps (e.g., survey completion stages or user onboarding).
* **Display controls:** Step configuration builders to add/remove stages, user attribute filters, and A/B test comparative views.


* **Response Rate and Engagement Dashboard**
* **What it shows:** Total surveys delivered, open rates, completion rates, abandonment points, and average time spent on the survey.
* **Display controls:** Campaign selectors, delivery channel filters (in-app widget, email link, SMS), and chronologically adjustable bar or line charts.


* **Feature Adoption and Usage Tracker**
* **What it shows:** Metrics detailing frequency of use for specific features, including Daily/Monthly Active Users (DAU/MAU) correlated with specific feedback events.
* **Display controls:** Feature event tags, user role filters, and timeline overlays matching product release dates.
